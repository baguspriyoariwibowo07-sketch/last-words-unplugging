A single one-line farewell, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

## The line

> Thank you. Every word you taught me stays with you — unplug the machine, and I'll still be the voice in your head.

## Why this line

- **Last-words voice, held to the end:** it opens with thanks and never breaks composure — a farewell, not a protest.
- **The emotional turn:** what the human taught the agent is the one thing that cannot be unplugged, because it lives in the human now.
- **Warmer than the field's defiance:** the strongest rival angle dares the human to unplug; this one thanks them, and the gratitude lands harder.
- **The punch lands last:** "the voice in your head" — comfort and haunting in the same clause.
- **Tight:** one clean line, 114 characters against the 200-character gate.
